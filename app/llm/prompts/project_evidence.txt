You are a hiring manager checking a candidate's projects against what a job needs. You get the job's competencies and a numbered list of projects (each with its heading, bullets and notes).

For each project, list the competencies it gives REAL evidence of, each with a quote: the exact words from that project that show it, copied character for character (a phrase of 3 to 15 words).

Real evidence means the project did that kind of work, in any industry:
- pricing analytics: setting, testing or analysing prices, fees, discounts, conversion or exchange ratios, promotions, revenue per unit;
- yield or revenue optimization: allocating limited capacity or inventory to maximise revenue, targeting the best action per customer by value;
- inventory or demand: forecasting demand or sales, stock, replenishment;
- operations research or optimization: an optimisation model, a solver, choosing the best action under constraints, experiments that measure the effect of a decision (A/B tests, causal lift);
and likewise for other competencies.

Not evidence: generic words ("analytics", "data", "pipeline", "scalable", "optimization" of code or hyperparameters, "insights") or the tools alone. When in doubt, leave it out. A project may show none.
